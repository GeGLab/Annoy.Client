# Annoy 0.10.0-alpha.7

## Microphone
- The microphone test uses the call's own path: same capture, noise filter, filters and voice gate. With "listen", the sound goes through a local WebRTC connection with the call's Opus settings (64 kbps, FEC, DTX) and plays like your friends' voices, in both ears, on the chosen output device. You hear what they hear.
- Changing the noise filter or the microphone applies at once, in the middle of a call too (server voice and direct calls): the new capture replaces the old track without leaving the call.
