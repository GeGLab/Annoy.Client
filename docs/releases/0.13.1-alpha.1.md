# Annoy 0.13.1-alpha.1

## Announcements
- Annoy notices and news, written in the Annoy cloud's admin panel, open once per device when the app opens with an Annoy account, on the local day they come out. Several at once show as tabs; "Fechar" closes them.
- Settings → Updates → Announcements lists every published announcement to read again.
- Each announcement is in the app's language when written in it, otherwise in English.

## Polls
- Announcements can carry polls: single choice or checkboxes, an optional close date, results after voting (or before, when the panel allows it). The vote belongs to the Annoy account and can be changed while the poll is open.
- The cloud stores only an anonymous marker per vote (HMAC of announcement, poll and account); nobody, not even the admins, sees who voted for what.

## Updated terms
- The Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy changed (version 2026-11-26) to cover poll votes. The app asks to accept them once ("Aceitar" / "Sair da conta"); until then the cloud gives no access to friends, DMs or polls.

## Song rooms
- Paste a YouTube or YouTube Music playlist link in the song request to pull its tracks into the queue (up to 200; the app says how many were left out).
- Room managers get a random mode and a one-time "Embaralhar fila" that can go back to the original order.

The News screen lists these changes.
